---
title: "Get started"
description: "Sign in to ility, add your first site, run an accessibility scan, and review the issues ility finds, grouped by severity, rule, and page."
---

This guide takes you from signing in to reviewing your first accessibility issues. In the ility app, the sites you monitor are called **Surfaces**.

## Before you begin

You need:

- An ility account
- The URL of a site you want to test, such as `https://example.com`

To add a site, you need to be a member or owner of your workspace. Viewers can see surfaces but can't add them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.ilitylabs.com](https://app.ilitylabs.com) and sign in.

    If this is your first time, ility asks you to create a workspace. A workspace holds the sites you monitor and the teammates you work with. Enter a **Workspace name** and select **Create workspace**.

    ility then asks **What site do you want to audit?** Enter your site's URL and select **Start my first scan**. ility adds the site and starts finding its pages, so you can skip to the **Run a full scan** step. To add a site later instead, select **Skip for now — take me to my sites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first site">
    In the sidebar, select **Surfaces**, then select **Add surface**. In an empty workspace, the button reads **Add your first surface**.

    On the **Add surface** page:

    1. In **Base URL**, enter the address of your site, such as `https://example.com`.
    2. Optional: in **Name**, enter a label your team will recognize, such as "Marketing Site". ility shows the URL under it.
    3. Under **How should we find pages?**, choose how ility finds your pages:
       - **Discover from the URL above** follows links from your URL. It works on any site and is the recommended option.
       - **Use `sitemap.xml`** reads your sitemap from its standard path. It's faster and more complete when your site has one.
    4. Select **Add surface and start crawl**. If you chose the sitemap, the button reads **Add surface and crawl sitemap**.

    ility opens the new surface and starts the crawl, which finds the pages on your site. The surface header shows the progress, such as "Crawling… 24 pages found". While the crawl runs, ility scans the first page it finds, so your first issues appear within about a minute.

    <Tip>
      To add the site without crawling it yet, select **Just register the surface — I'll start a crawl later**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a full scan">
    The first automatic scan only covers one page. To scan every page the crawl found, select **Run scan** in the surface header.

    The header shows the scan's progress, such as "Scanning 12 of 140 pages". You can pause, resume, or cancel the scan from the same place.

    If some pages haven't been scanned yet, the **Overview** tab offers a button to scan only those, such as **Scan 18 unscanned pages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your results">
    Open the **Overview** tab of your surface. It starts with one sentence on how the site is doing and its score, followed by what to fix next, the scan history, and a breakdown of issues by WCAG criterion and severity.

    The score measures WCAG conformance only. It weighs each failing rule by its severity and by how many elements fail it. Best-practice issues are tracked separately and don't affect the score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review issues">
    Open the **Issues** tab of your surface. Next to **View issues by**, choose how to group them:

    - **Issues** lists each issue with its severity, status, and the pages it affects.
    - **Rules** groups issues by the accessibility rule they break.
    - **Pages** lists your pages with the issues on each one.

    Use the **Severity** filter to focus on **Critical**, **Serious**, **Moderate**, or **Minor** issues, and the **Status** filter to show **Open**, **Resolved**, **Won't fix**, or **False positive** issues. Select an issue to see the details.

    To see issues across every surface in your workspace, select **Issues** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>
