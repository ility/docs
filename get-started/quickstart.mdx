---

<Note>
  ility is in early access. If the sign-up page asks you to join the waitlist, add your email and we'll send you an invitation when your account is ready.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.ilitylabs.com](https://app.ilitylabs.com) and choose **Sign up**. You can sign up with Google, with GitHub, or with an email address and password. If you use email, we send a code to confirm the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    A workspace holds the sites you monitor and the teammates you work with. Enter a name, such as your company or client name, and choose **Create workspace**.

    Every new workspace starts with a free 14-day Pro trial. You don't need a card to start it.

    <Tip>
      If someone already invited you to their workspace, you'll see it here instead. Select it to join.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your site">
    ility asks **What site do you want to audit?** Enter the address of the site, such as `https://example.com`, and choose **Start my first scan**.

    ility adds the site, starts mapping its pages, and scans the first page right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the first findings arrive">
    You land on the site's overview. The page shows the crawl as it finds pages, and the first findings usually appear within a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the rest of the site">
    When the crawl finishes, the **Automated coverage** card shows how many pages have been scanned. Choose **Scan N unscanned pages** to scan everything the crawl found.
  </Step>
</Steps>
