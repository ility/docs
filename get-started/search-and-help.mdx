## Search

Search jumps to any surface or place in the app by name. Open it with **Search** in the header, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux.

- With nothing typed, search shows **Recent**, the places you opened last, and **Go to**, every place in the app.
- Start typing to find matching surfaces and places, such as a surface's **Issues** or the **API keys** settings.
- Use the arrow keys to move through the results, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open one. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close search.

Recent places are saved in your browser, so they don't follow you to another computer. Search finds surfaces and places in the app. It doesn't search issue or page content yet. To find an issue, use the filters on a surface's **Issues** list.

If the shortcut doesn't open search, close any open dialog first, or choose **Search** in the header.

## Get help

Choose **Help** in the header for three options:

- **Documentation** opens this site.
- **Report a problem** tells us what went wrong and what you expected.
- **Send feedback** tells us what works, what doesn't, or what you wish Ility did.

Write up to 4,000 characters and choose **Send**. Your message comes to our support team with your name and email, so we can reply, and the address of the page you were on. A problem report also includes the last error the app recorded and, unless your browser asks not to be tracked, a recording of your session with all text and images hidden.

If a message can't be sent, your text stays in the box. You can also email [support@ilitylabs.com](mailto:support@ilitylabs.com).
