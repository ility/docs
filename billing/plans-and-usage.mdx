Every workspace has one plan. The plan decides what the workspace can do, and its size decides how many surfaces it holds and how many URLs it can scan each month. For prices and the numbers behind each size, see the [pricing page](https://ilitylabs.com/pricing).

Ility has four plans:

- **Free**: one surface, for trying Ility.
- **Plus**: for one organization testing its own sites.
- **Pro**: for agencies and teams that look after many sites.
- **Enterprise**: for more surfaces, single sign-on, or custom terms. It's set up with our sales team, not on the **Billing** page.

Each plan includes everything in the plan before it.

Pro allows more API keys than Plus. Free workspaces have room for fewer people than paid plans. The [pricing page](https://ilitylabs.com/pricing) has the details.

When your plan doesn't include something, Ility says which plan does. Through the API, the request fails with `403` and a `reason`. Most plan gates use `reason: "plan_required"`, with `required_plan` naming the plan. Starting scans uses `api_triggers_not_entitled`, sharing a report uses `report_sharing_not_entitled`, and a workspace at its key limit gets `api_key_limit_reached`.

## The Pro trial

Every new workspace starts with a free 14-day trial of Pro. You don't need a card to start it. A banner at the top of the app counts down the days that are left.

When the trial ends, the workspace moves to Free unless an owner chooses a plan. The **Billing** page recommends a plan based on how the workspace used its trial. Your surfaces, scans, and issues are kept.

## Scanned URLs

Each plan includes a number of scanned URLs a month. Scanning one page or one document once uses one scanned URL.

These never count:

- Finding pages, with a discovery crawl or a sitemap.
- Availability checks.
- Pages that fail to load. Ility gives those back when the scan finishes.

Usage resets on the first day of each month, UTC. Owners get an email when the workspace has used 75%, 90%, and 100% of its URLs for the month.

### When the month's URLs run out

Unless overages are on, new scans wait until the reset, and automatic scans pause. Re-checking a single issue still works for a few URLs past the limit, so you can confirm a fix. Through the API, a scan that needs more URLs than are left fails with `402` and `reason: "scan_allowance_reached"`.

To keep scanning, an owner can do one of these on the **Billing** page:

- Add **Extra URL blocks** to the plan.
- Under **When you reach your limit**, choose **Allow overages up to a monthly budget**. Ility then buys extra URLs as you need them, up to that budget, and invoices them after the month ends. Overages are available on paid plans.
- Change to a bigger plan or size.

## Change your plan

Only owners see **Billing** in the sidebar. The **Billing and usage** page shows the current plan, how many URLs each surface used this month, and the overage setting.

- Under **Choose a plan**, pick Plus or Pro, a size, and monthly or yearly billing, then choose **Continue to checkout**. Once you have a plan, choose **Change plan** to change it.
- A bigger plan takes effect right away, and the difference is invoiced now. A smaller plan takes effect at renewal.
- Choose **Manage payment and invoices** to update your card or download invoices.

Changing plans never deletes anything. If a workspace moves to a plan without a feature, what it made stays readable. For example, existing evidence packets can still be downloaded. If it has more surfaces than its new size holds, archive surfaces until it fits. Archived surfaces keep their history.

For Enterprise, email [sales+enterprise@ilitylabs.com](mailto:sales+enterprise@ilitylabs.com).

## When a payment fails

The app shows **Payment failed** at the top of every page, and owners see **Update payment**. Update the card to keep monitoring running.

- After 14 days overdue, automatic scans and availability checks stop.
- After 30 days overdue, no new scans, crawls, or re-checks can start, and no surfaces can be added.

Your data is kept the whole time. Everything starts again once the payment goes through.

## Free workspaces that aren't used

If nobody signs in to a Free workspace for 60 days, its automatic scans pause. They start again the next time someone signs in.
