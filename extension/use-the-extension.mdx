---
title: "Use the extension"
description: "See known issues on the live page, scan the current page state, and jump to failing elements."
---

## Open the panel

Open DevTools on the page you want to check: <kbd>⌥⌘I</kbd> on macOS, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> on Windows and Linux. Choose the **ility** tab. If you don't see it, look under the **»** menu at the end of the DevTools tab row.

The panel follows the page as you navigate, including in single-page apps.

## See the issues ility already knows about

When the site is one of your workspace's surfaces, the panel lists **Known open issues** for the page, worst first. For each issue you can:

- Select it to outline the element on the page.
- Choose **Inspect** to select the element in the DevTools Elements panel.
- Choose the issue number to open the issue in ility.

Issues that ility saw on another page of the same site are listed too, but they can't be highlighted here.

The panel tells you when it can't match the page:

- **This site isn't in your ility workspace yet.** Choose **Add it now** to add it as a surface.
- **ility knows this site but hasn't seen this page.** The page is probably behind a login or in the middle of a flow. Run a local scan to check it.

## Scan the page as it is right now

Under **Scan this page state**, choose **Run local scan**. The extension runs axe-core against the live page in your browser, so it checks whatever is on screen: an open dialog, an expanded menu, a form showing errors, or a page behind a login.

The results list every finding with its severity, rule, and element. Select a finding to outline it on the page, or choose **Inspect** to open it in the Elements panel. When the page is already tracked by ility, the summary tells you which findings are **likely known** and which are **new**. Findings that fall outside a WCAG AA scan, such as AAA or best-practice rules, are labeled **AAA/best-practice**.

<Tip>
  Local scans stay in your browser. They don't create issues in ility. Use them to check a fix before you ship it, or to find problems on pages the crawler can't reach.
</Tip>

When the page is tracked, choose **Clear highlights** under the known issues to remove the outlines. Reloading the page also clears them.
