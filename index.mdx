ility is accessibility testing for websites. Accessibility issues stop people from using a site: a button a screen reader can't name, text too faint to read, or a form you can't complete with a keyboard. ility helps you find those issues and track them until they're fixed.

## What ility does

- **Crawls your site.** ility follows links from the URL you give it, or reads your `sitemap.xml`, to find the pages on your site.
- **Scans every page.** ility checks each page against WCAG, the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, and against accessibility best practices.
- **Groups the issues.** The same problem on many pages shows up as one issue, with a severity and the pages it affects.
- **Scores each site.** Each site gets a score based on how many WCAG rules fail, how severe they are, and how many elements fail them.
- **Tracks issues over time.** Each scan updates the issue list, so you can see what's new, what's resolved, and how the score changes.
- **Exports evidence.** You can export evidence of your testing for audits and compliance reviews.

Automated testing finds many accessibility issues, but not all of them. Some checks still need a person to judge whether the result works for real users.

## Who ility is for

- **Web teams.** Developers, designers, QA, and content writers who want to find and fix accessibility issues on the sites they build.
- **Compliance and product leads.** People who need to know how accessible their sites are, how that changes over time, and how to show it.
- **Agencies and their clients.** Teams that test sites for clients can invite each client into the workspace with access to only their own sites. See [Manage members and client access](/members).
