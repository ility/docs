A report sums up one full-site scan in a form you can hand to someone else: a client, a prospect, or your own leadership. It counts the issues the scan found, groups them by cause, and shows what changed since an earlier scan.

Every completed full-site scan has a report. Page scans don't, and neither does a scan that couldn't load any page.

## Open a report

There are two ways in:

- Open a surface and choose **Reports** in the sidebar. **Status** is the latest full-site scan, compared with the one before it. **Baseline** is the first full-site scan, where the site started, with nothing to compare it with.
- Open any completed full-site scan and choose **View report**.

Reports appear after a surface's first full-site scan finishes.

## What a report shows

- **Summary**: how many issues fail a WCAG success criterion, and what share of the automated checks passed.
- **Issues at a glance**: counts by severity, and how many pages are affected.
- **WCAG failures by cause**: issues grouped by the rule that found them, with the success criteria each group fails and example pages.
- **Best-practice findings**: issues that don't map to a WCAG success criterion.
- **Change since** an earlier scan: which issues no longer appear, which are new, and how failed checks and affected places moved.
- **How this was measured**: the scan's date, the pages it tested, and what automated testing can and can't find.

Issues you've marked **False positive** are left out of every issue count and group, so a report never repeats a finding your team has rejected. Check counts still include them, because a check is the raw result of one rule on one page. Fixing an issue later doesn't change an earlier scan's report: each report shows what that scan found.

<Note>
Automated testing covers only part of WCAG. A report with no WCAG failures says that automated testing found none. It never says a site meets WCAG.
</Note>

## Compare with a different scan

A report compares with the previous full-site scan by default. To compare with another one, choose it under **Compare with**. You can pick any earlier full-site scan of the surface made with the same scan engine.

## Save a report as a PDF

For a clean PDF, create a share link and choose **Download PDF** on the shared page. Printing the report inside the app also prints the app's menus and controls. See [Share a report](/reports/share-a-report).
