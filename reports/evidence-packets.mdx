title: "Evidence packets"
description: "Download a dated record of what Ility tested on a surface, what it found, and when each issue was fixed."
---

Sometimes someone asks you to show your accessibility work: a procurement questionnaire, an internal audit, or a customer's legal team. If Ility has been scanning a surface, it already holds that record. An evidence packet turns it into two files you can hand over:

- A CSV issue register, with one row per issue.
- A PDF evidence document.

Evidence packets need the Plus or Pro plan. See [Plans and usage](/billing/plans-and-usage).

## What a packet covers

A packet covers one surface and everything Ility knows about it when you generate it:

- The scan record: which scans ran and how many pages they tested.
- Each issue's history, with timestamps: when it was found, when its status changed, and whether a later scan confirmed the fix.
- How issues break down by severity and by WCAG success criterion.
- Automated coverage.

A packet reports automated results only. It doesn't include a manual audit, testing with assistive technology, or human review of the WCAG criteria that automated testing can't check. Every page says so. A passing automated result isn't a conformance claim, and a packet isn't a VPAT.

## Generate a packet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the surface's evidence">
    Open a surface and choose **Evidence** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the packet">
    Choose **Generate evidence packet**. Ility builds it in the background, which usually takes under a minute. You can leave the page while it works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the files">
    When **Evidence packet ready** appears, choose **Download CSV** or **Download PDF**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Anyone who can open the surface can generate and download its packets, including viewers.

## Download an earlier packet

**Recent packets** lists the surface's last five packets, with when each was generated and how many issues, scans, and pages it covers. Choose **CSV** or **PDF** next to a packet to download it again without building a new one.

If a download fails, the packet may no longer be available. Generate a new one.

Packets you already generated stay downloadable if the workspace moves to a plan without evidence packets.

## If a packet doesn't finish

Most packets finish in under a minute. If one takes more than five minutes, the page says it's taking longer than expected, and the packet appears in **Recent packets** when it's ready.

A packet that still hasn't finished after about 90 minutes won't finish. Ility marks it **failed** in **Recent packets** and alerts the Ility team. Generate a new one.
