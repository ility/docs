title: "Surface settings"
description: "Choose the WCAG target, engines, page rendering, crawl behavior, scan schedule, and availability checks for each surface."
---

Open a surface and choose **Settings** in the sidebar. Members and owners can change settings. Viewers see a view-only notice instead.

## Scan configuration

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The label shown above the surface's URL. |
| **WCAG conformance target** | The WCAG version and level scans test against, such as **WCAG 2.2 AA**. New surfaces start at WCAG 2.2 AA. |
| **Environment** | An optional label, such as `production` or `staging`, to tell similar surfaces apart. |
| **Enabled engines** | Which scan engines run against this surface. When none are selected, every engine available to the workspace runs. |

The WCAG target decides which rules count. A 2.1 AA target runs every rule mapped to a level A or AA success criterion in WCAG 2.0 or 2.1. Best-practice rules run on every scan, whatever the target. See [What a scan checks](/scans/what-a-scan-checks).

## Page rendering

Use these settings for a site that builds its pages in the browser, such as a single-page app or an e-learning course. Before it reads a page, Ility waits for some visible text, for up to 10 seconds.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Wait for element** | A CSS selector, such as `main .lesson`, for an element that appears once a page has rendered. Ility waits for it on every page instead of for visible text, so choose an element every page has. A page without it waits the full time. |
| **Longest wait (seconds)** | From 1 to 30. Leave it blank for 10. |

## Crawl and scheduling

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Include query parameters when crawling** | Treat URLs that differ only by query string, such as `/products?page=2`, as separate pages. Leave this off for most sites. |
| **Scheduled scanning** | Scan the surface automatically, so new issues and fixes show up without anyone pressing **Run scan**. Choose **How often**: **Monthly**, **Weekly**, or **Daily**. Your plan sets the most frequent choice. Automatic scans count toward the workspace's scanned URLs. They pause when those run out or when a payment is overdue. |
| **Availability checks** | Check every five minutes that the homepage and key pages answer, and tell members when the site goes down or comes back. Availability checks need the Plus or Pro plan, and they don't count toward scanned URLs. |

Your plan sets the most frequent schedule and whether availability checks are available. See [Plans and usage](/billing/plans-and-usage).

Choose **Save changes** when you're done.

## Scan login

The **Scan login** card signs Ility in to your site so crawls and scans reach pages behind a login. It saves separately from the settings above, with its own **Save login** button. See [Scan pages behind a login](/surfaces/scan-behind-a-login).

## Delete a surface

**Delete domain…** in the **Danger zone** at the bottom of Settings permanently deletes the surface with all of its pages, scans, and issues. It can't be undone. Only workspace owners can delete a surface, and you confirm by typing the surface's short name (its slug, such as `example-com`).
